Servflow counts everything your agents use in credits. This page explains what a credit buys, how a run's cost is worked out, what each plan includes, and what happens when your team runs out, so you can predict your bill. To add credits, see [Top up credits](/guides/top-up-credits).

## What a credit is

One credit is $0.001, so 1,000 credits is $1. Your team has one balance, shared by every agent and every person on the team. You see it under **Settings** > **Billing and usage**.

## What a run costs

A run costs two things:

- **The AI, at cost.** When a sub agent uses Servflow's built-in model, each dollar the model costs is 1,055 credits: 1,000 credits for the dollar, plus the 5.5% fee that OpenRouter, the service Servflow reaches models through, charges. A run's AI cost is rounded up to a whole credit.
- **The run fee.** Every run costs 4 credits, charged when the run starts.

For example, a run whose model calls cost $0.01 uses 11 credits of AI and the 4-credit run fee, 15 credits in all.

A test run you start with **Run** in the agent editor pays no run fee. It still pays for the AI it uses.

The model your agents run on is set for the whole team under **Settings** > **Team** > **Model**. A cheaper model makes the same credits last longer. For how the built-in model works, see [Providers](/concepts/providers).

### Assist messages

Each message you send to Assist costs 2 credits, whether or not Assist answers it. Assist runs on a model Servflow chooses, and its AI cost comes out of your credits at the same rate as a run's.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 4,000 once, when the team is created |
| Paid | $10 a month | 10,000 a month |
| Starter | $25 a month | 26,000 a month |
| Pro | $50 a month | 55,000 a month |

Every plan has the same 4-credit run fee and the same AI at cost, with unlimited people and agents. The plans differ in what they allow at once:

| Plan | Run time limit | Runs at once | Runs a minute | Traces kept |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 5 minutes a run | 3 | 10 | 7 days |
| Paid | 15 minutes a run | 10 | 100 | 14 days |
| Starter | 30 minutes a run | 20 | 250 | 30 days |
| Pro | 90 minutes a run | 50 | 500 | 90 days |

A team can try the Paid plan free for 14 days, once.

## Your own keys

A sub agent can run on your own account with a model company instead of the built-in model. You add the key under **Settings** > **Your own keys** and choose it as the sub agent's provider. The model company then bills you for that sub agent's AI, and Servflow charges only the 4-credit run fee. See [Manage LLM providers](/guides/manage-llm-providers).

## When credits renew and expire

Credits come from two places, and they expire differently:

- **Plan credits** arrive each time your paid plan renews, on your billing date, not at the start of the calendar month. Plan credits you have not spent expire at the next renewal, when the new month's credits arrive. When you leave a paid plan, its unspent credits expire.
- **Credits you buy** with a top-up never expire, and stay when you change or leave your plan. Free's 4,000 credits count as bought credits: they never expire and stay when you choose a plan.

**Billing and usage** shows how many of your credits are plan credits that expire at the next renewal, and the renewal date.

## When credits run out

Nothing is billed past your plan unless you top up.

When your team runs out of credits:

- New runs of every agent are refused.
- A run that is already going fails if it needs more of the built-in model.
- Assist does not take new messages.

Runs start again as soon as credits return: when a top-up's payment clears, when your paid plan renews, or when you choose a plan.
